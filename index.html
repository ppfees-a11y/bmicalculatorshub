How BMI is calculated

BMI equals weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. For US measurements, the calculator converts pounds to kilograms and inches to meters before calculating. The result is rounded to one decimal place for display.

For example, a person who weighs 80 kg and is 1.80 m tall has a BMI of approximately 24.7 (80 ÷ 1.80²). This calculation describes a weight-to-height ratio, not a direct body-fat percentage.

Why use this BMI tool?

You can switch between US and metric measurements, instantly see the BMI category, inspect the mathematical weight interval corresponding to BMI 18.5–24.9, and compare reference points visually. All calculations happen in your browser; the tool does not require an account or ask for a name.

Important limitations

BMI cannot distinguish fat from muscle or account for where fat is distributed. An athletic person may have a higher BMI without excess body fat. People with certain health conditions may need different assessments. BMI-for-age measures are used for children and teens; this calculator is not intended for them.

Do not use the mathematical weight range as an instruction to change weight. For personalized health questions, consult a qualified health professional.
