Standard adult BMI categories: below 18.5 (underweight), 18.5 to below 25 (healthy weight), 25 to below 30 (overweight), and 30 or above (obesity, with three classes). These are screening categories, not diagnoses.

For ages 2–19, age- and sex-specific BMI percentiles are required; do not use this adult calculator to classify children.
