About

About BMICalculatorsHub, its educational purpose, and its independent publishing approach.

Our purpose

BMICalculatorsHub offers a free, accessible adult BMI calculator supported by plain-language explanations, worked examples, and a transparent calculation method. We aim to make the arithmetic easy to inspect, not to replace individualized clinical advice.

Editorial approach

The formula, unit conversions, category definitions, and limitations are stated openly. We aim to keep tools useful and correct and to distinguish mathematical reference points from medical recommendations.

Independence and advertising

This site is independently published. Advertising may be displayed to support free access. Advertisements do not change the BMI formula or the numeric results. We do not claim affiliation with the CDC or any government agency.
