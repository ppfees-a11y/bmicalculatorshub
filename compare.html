Compare two adult BMI measurements

Compare BMI values at a fixed height using two weights. Useful for understanding the arithmetic behind a change in BMI. This is not a weight-loss target or health assessment.

Adults 20+ only. Do not use for pregnancy or children. BMI does not distinguish fat from muscle or changes in fluid.

Two-weight comparison

Understanding the result

For the same height, BMI changes in direct proportion to weight. A change in BMI is not proof of a change in body fat or health. Fluid shifts, muscle changes, measurement error and other factors can matter.

Use consistent units and measurements when comparing results. Consult a healthcare professional for interpretation, particularly if your weight changes unexpectedly.
