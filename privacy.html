Privacy Policy

Privacy details for the free BMI calculator and potential advertising.

Calculator inputs

Height and weight values are processed in your browser to display BMI results. We do not ask for an account and the calculator does not transmit these inputs to our own calculation server.

Hosting and technical information

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting and browser systems may process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata as part of delivering pages.

Advertising and cookies

We may use Google AdSense to support this free site. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, and personalization depending on applicable consent choices. Learn about Google data practices at Google advertising policies.

Your choices

You can manage cookies through browser settings and Google ad preferences. This site does not require you to submit health details to use the BMI tool.

Changes

We may update this policy as services and requirements change. Review this page periodically for the latest published version.
